I've lived with the same roommate for three years now, somehow. We were suitemates freshman
year, and once we moved off campus to save money, we became roommates in the cheapest room
in the house. I've been unfortunately cursed with getting to know her well.

My roommate is a brilliant math major who bounces between intensive imposter syndrome and
extreme arrogance. Despite completing two REUs by junior year (and headed into her third REU),
she sometimes traps me into three hour long rants about how she will never make it into grad
school and that she will be forever poor. At other times, she will go on, with much contempt,
about how incompetent certain classmates are and how she wishes group projects didn't exist.
(True on that last one, but a problem if you're struggling to work with every group ever.)

It seems that she holds everyone, including herself, to an impossibly high intellectual 
standard and if one doesn't meet that standard, they deserve nothing. In fact, I think that
she thinks on a subconscious level that such people are worth less, 
and if we were to label people with dollar signs, such individuals would be on sale.

Of course, when I confront her about this worldview, she will instantly deny it due to the
extreme nature of this verbalized perspective, but I think it's something she will come to
understand about herself someday (hopefully).

It's difficult for me to assess my true and original worth, as a clearance shelf item. I
highly dislilke people telling me I struggle with imposter syndrome, when I am indeed an imposter.
